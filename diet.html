GENERAL DIET GUIDE

Eat to support your training.

This is general educational content, not a personalised medical or nutrition prescription.

Weight Gain

  • Breakfast: oats, milk, eggs/vegetarian protein, fruit
  • Mid-morning: nuts + fruit
  • Lunch: roti/rice, dal, vegetables, protein source
  • Evening: curd/fruit or balanced snack
  • Dinner: roti/rice + vegetables + protein

Weight Loss

  • Breakfast: protein-rich meal + fruit
  • Mid-morning: fruit/curd
  • Lunch: vegetables + dal/protein + controlled carbs
  • Evening: light snack
  • Dinner: vegetables + protein

Muscle Support

  • Breakfast: protein + whole grains
  • Pre-workout: easy-to-digest carbohydrate
  • Post-workout: protein-rich meal/snack
  • Lunch: balanced plate
  • Dinner: protein + vegetables + suitable carbs
For medical conditions or therapeutic diets, consult a qualified healthcare professional/dietitian.
